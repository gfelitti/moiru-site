    Our vision

    our_vision_anchor

    Climate change is the most critical issue of our time. Solutions must be implemented today, not tomorrow - and fast. After years of supporting companies, NGOs, and other organizations in extracting actionable insights from raw data, Novelo Data has established Moiru—a dedicated branch of our consultancy focused exclusively on helping organizations with an environmental and climate change focus to harness data and artificial intelligence in creating practical, data-driven approaches that lead to measurable environmental impact.

    Who we are

    Partner

    Flávio Nakasato Cação

    Flávio Nakasato Cação has a decade of experience with data ecosystems in technology companies of various sizes, working as an analyst, engineer, and data scientist. He was a researcher at the Center for Artificial Intelligence (C4AI) and the Center for Data Science (C2D) at the University of São Paulo (USP). He holds a Bachelor's degree in Physics from USP and a Master's degree in Machine Learning from the Polytechnic School of the same university.

    Partner

    Guilherme Felitti

    Guilherme Felitti has +13 years of experience working with data in journalistic and tech companies. He co-founded Novelo Data to lead environmental data projects for clients including Funbio, Greenpeace, and Talanoa. Moiru represents his next venture, created together with Flávio Nakasato. He holds a master's degree in Intelligence Technologies from PUC-SP and is currently enrolled in a Master in Business Analytics program at Emory University, in Atlanta.
